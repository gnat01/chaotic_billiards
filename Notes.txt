The classic chaotic billiards problem

- Showing periodic trajectories
- Showing sensitive dependence to initial conditions and chaos
- Varying geometries : Square, circular, triangular, rectangular, diamond, irregular
- Going from elastic to inelastic scattering so in the latter the billiards ball loses energy as it bounces and even when it moves along the table
- Tracking a lsew of metrics - velocity, position, energy in system, number of bounces per unit time, Lyapunov exponent, fractal dimension of trajectory traced
